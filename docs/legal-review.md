# Legal copy review

Last reviewed: 28 July 2026 (`America/La_Paz`)

## Scope

Phase 10 replaced the short placeholder copy with operationally specific Spanish
purchase conditions and privacy disclosures. The pages now explain:

- the 15-minute reservation and minute-25 payment-report window;
- authoritative payment confirmation and price expiration;
- availability revalidation;
- cancellation before purchase, late-payment handling, and QR refunds;
- guest versus permanent-account data;
- private payment evidence and technical providers;
- retention purpose and the WhatsApp channel for access or correction requests.

## Reference basis

The review considered the following official Bolivian sources:

- [Law No. 453 — General Law on the Rights of Users and Consumers](https://www.gacetaoficialdebolivia.gob.bo/normas/buscar/453)
- [Law No. 164 — General Law on Telecommunications and Information and Communication Technologies](https://www.gacetaoficialdebolivia.gob.bo/normas/view/139394)
- [Political Constitution of the State](https://www.gacetaoficialdebolivia.gob.bo/app/webroot/archivos/constitucion.pdf)

## Review gate

The visible copy is marked `Versión para revisión legal`. It is an accurate
description of the implemented workflow, but it is not a substitute for review by a
Bolivian legal professional before production launch. The final business identity,
physical or legal contact details, exact retention periods, delivery responsibility,
and any mandatory tax or consumer disclosures must be confirmed before Phase 12.
